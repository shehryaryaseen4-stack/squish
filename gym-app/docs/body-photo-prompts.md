# Prompts for the 24 body type pictures

Use these with an AI image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Designer, Leonardo,
Ideogram…). Before using the pictures in an app you sell, check that the tool's
terms allow commercial use.

**To keep all 24 pictures alike:** make them in one chat, and from the second
one on, write "Same person, same style, pose, clothes and background as the
previous image, only the body changes:" before the prompt.

## Base prompt

Copy this and put a body line from below in place of `[BODY]`:

> Full-body front view of a [PERSON], standing straight facing the camera, arms
> relaxed at the sides, whole body visible from head to feet, centered. Body:
> [BODY]. Wearing [CLOTHES], no logos or text. Semi-realistic digital painting,
> dramatic gym lighting, blurred modern gym in the background. Tall portrait
> image, 3:5.

- Men: `PERSON` = `30-year-old man with short dark hair`, `CLOTHES` = `a plain grey tank top and black gym shorts`
- Women: `PERSON` = `30-year-old woman with dark hair in a ponytail`, `CLOTHES` = `a plain fitted sports tank top and black leggings`

(Change the age, hair or skin to suit your customers.)

Save each picture with the file name shown, then send them over. They are
cropped to 480×800 and put in `public/shapes/`.
