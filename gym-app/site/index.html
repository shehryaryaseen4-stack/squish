Questions

Frequently asked

Which fingerprint machines work?

Machines that can send attendance to a server ("ADMS", "Cloud server" or "Push"): most ZKTeco, eSSL/Identix and ZKTeco ZLink models, and Hikvision face terminals with HTTP Listening. Each member's number in the app is their user ID on the machine. If you're unsure, message us the model and we'll tell you.

What's the difference between Online and Windows?

Online keeps your gym on our servers: use it from anywhere and members can open their app from home. Windows runs everything on your own front desk computer and needs no internet, but members' phones only connect while they're on the gym Wi-Fi.

Can I use my own currency and language for messages?

Yes. Pick your country when you sign up and your currency, time zone and payment methods are set. You can change them, and the payment methods, any time in Settings.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Subscribe any time during the trial and nothing changes. If you don't, the app is paused, and everything you entered is kept safe until you subscribe.

Can my staff use it without seeing the money?

Yes. Give each person their own PIN and switch on only what they need, for example check-in and adding members, without fees, reports or expenses.

Is my data safe?

Each gym's data is kept separately, PINs are stored encrypted, and data is backed up. On the Windows version the data stays on your computer, and you can back it up with one click.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. See our refund policy.
