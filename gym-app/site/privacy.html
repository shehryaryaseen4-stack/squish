Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This is a starting template. Have it checked for your country before you rely on it.

This policy explains what collects when you use and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: gym name, owner email and phone, country.
  • Gym data you enter: members' names, phone numbers, photos, attendance, payments, workout plans and notes. We process this on your behalf; you decide what is entered.
  • Payment details: handled by Paddle, our payment provider. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP addresses and errors, used to keep the Service secure and working.

How we use it

To provide and support the Service, to keep it secure, to send account and billing messages, and to improve it. We don't sell personal data and don't use members' data for advertising.

Storage and security

Each gym's data is stored separately. PINs and passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Data is backed up regularly. Windows licence users keep their gym data on their own computer.

Sharing

Only with service providers who help run the Service (hosting and payments), when the law requires it, or with your permission.

How long we keep it

While your account is active, and up to 90 days after it ends unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
