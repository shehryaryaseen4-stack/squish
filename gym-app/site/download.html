Download the apps

Online gyms can use any device in a web browser too. These apps make it feel at home on each one.

Setting up

  1. Windows: open the downloaded file. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. Then choose Online account and type your gym's web address, or Windows licence and enter your licence key.
  2. Android: open the file on the phone and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser when asked. On first start, type your gym's web address.
  3. Entry screen: connect the TV with HDMI, press Windows + P and choose Extend, then click Entry screen (TV) in the app.
